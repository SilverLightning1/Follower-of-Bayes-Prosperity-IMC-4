# Follower of Bayes - IMC Prosperity 4

Team Follower of Bayes' submission to the IMC Prosperity 4 Trading Challenge.

## Competition

IMC Prosperity 4 attracted:

- 30,703 participants
- 18,803 teams
- 1,549 universities
- 117 countries

The competition consisted of five rounds of algorithmic trading challenges requiring participants to develop and refine trading strategies under dynamic market conditions.

## Team

## Results

- Advanced to the finals
- Reached the Top 2% of teams before the final rounds
- Finished in the Top 8% globally

## About

Throughout the competition, our team built and iterated on Python-based trading algorithms, using quantitative analysis and backtesting to evaluate and improve our strategies across multiple rounds.

This repository intentionally does not contain the competition source code. Instead, it serves as a brief overview of our participation and experience in IMC Prosperity 4.
