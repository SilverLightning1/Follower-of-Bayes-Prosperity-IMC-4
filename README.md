## 💻 About

Throughout the competition, our team built and iterated on Python-based trading algorithms, using quantitative analysis and backtesting to evaluate and improve our strategies across multiple rounds.

This repository intentionally does **not** contain the competition source code. Instead, it serves as a brief overview of our participation and experience in IMC Prosperity 4.

## 📚 What We Learned

Participating in IMC Prosperity 4 was an incredible learning experience that pushed us to think beyond textbook algorithms and adapt to dynamic market environments.

Some of our key takeaways were:

- **Rapid experimentation is crucial.** The ability to quickly prototype, test, and iterate on ideas often mattered more than finding the perfect strategy on the first attempt.

- **Markets are adaptive.** Strategies that perform well initially may lose their edge as market conditions evolve, making flexibility and continuous refinement essential.

- **Risk management matters as much as profitability.** Aggressive trading can generate high returns, but balancing profit opportunities with inventory and exposure management is critical for long-term performance.

- **Backtesting has limitations.** Historical performance provides useful insights, but robust strategies must generalize to unseen market conditions and changing participant behavior.

- **Teamwork amplifies results.** Collaborating under time pressure taught us the importance of dividing responsibilities, challenging assumptions, and combining different perspectives when solving complex problems.

Beyond the rankings, Prosperity 4 strengthened our skills in quantitative thinking, algorithm design, data-driven decision making, and working effectively as a team in a highly competitive environment.
